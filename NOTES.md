## Terminology (canonical)

- **BOW** — Budget of Work, the DepEd curriculum breakdown (PDF).
- **Material** — one teaching-material product on the store; generated from a BOW entry.
- **Draft** — the generated artifact(s) for one material before review.
- **Review** — editor checking draft against the 3 checks.
- **The 3 checks** — BOW competency alignment / factual correctness / formatting.
- **Publish** — making a reviewed material live on the store.
- **Fulfillment** — automatic: mark paid + email signed R2 link.
- **Artifact type** — lesson plan, slides, exam, or worksheet. One BOW entry can make one or multiple of each.
